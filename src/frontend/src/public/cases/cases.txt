15 FEATURED CASES
=================

These 15 cases are pinned to `featuredOrder` 1-15 in the `Case` model
(src/backend/prisma/schema/patient.prisma) and are always served first to a
new game session, in the exact order listed below, before the game falls
back to its existing difficulty-tiered random selection
(selectNextCase, src/backend/src/services/round.ts).

Selection rule: featuredOrder N == realCases.ts case-0N.png, i.e. the first
15 cases the player plays are exactly case-01.png through case-15.png from
realCases.ts, in that exact sequential order (1→1, 2→2, ... 15→15). No
fictionalCases.ts entries are featured. Each of these 15 patients also has a
dedicated mock portrait at src/frontend/public/portraits/portrait-0N.png
(a simple placeholder image showing the number N), wired through
Patient.portraitImageUrl in src/backend/src/db/seed.ts and rendered in the
Chat component via useRound().case.patient.portraitImageUrl. All other
patients (the remaining realCases.ts entries and all fictionalCases.ts
entries) fall back to cycling through the 10 generic AI-portrait files in
src/frontend/public/patient-portraits/.

15 entries, 4 unique diagnoses: melanoma (x12), dysplastic-nevus (x1),
basal-cell-carcinoma (x1), squamous-cell-carcinoma (x1). Diversity is
limited by design here — the first 15 cases must be real cases matching
case-01..case-15 in strict image-number order, and melanoma dominates the
early entries of realCases.ts.
